DATA & INTERPRETATION

What you are looking at

The ground features come from Map 1's GeoPackage. Runway thresholds and width references come from SANS AIP AD 2.12. All 24 SID sequences and 50 fix coordinates were transcribed from the six retained AIP tabulation pages dated 17 September 2026.

Published vs illustrated

Fix positions, leg order and displayed restrictions are published data. The runway roll point, take-off position, climb between fixes, animation speed and aircraft are illustrative. Straight connectors visit the fixes but do not reconstruct CF/TF path behaviour, fly-over turns, ATC vectors or aircraft dynamics.

Ground is flat. Buildings use actual footprints with illustrative 12 m / 20 m heights; no surveyed terrain or building elevations are claimed. Vertical emphasis changes the display, not the altitude readout. Flight levels retain their original labels; nominal hundreds of feet are used for the animation without a weather-dependent conversion.

Source issues retained

MIGDA 3X: JN274 is absent from the 34L coordinate list; its coordinate is cross-referenced from the same-amendment 34C/34R lists. EGREP 2W: the canonical A1 reference places the 13,000 ft upper restriction at JN520. The earlier map transcription has been corrected.

This is an exploration prototype. Local recorded-flight playback uses Wingbits observations when available. SID scores describe geometric agreement, not assigned clearances. In this local copy the Noise panel shows the departure-noise results of 1 Oct 2026 (OEJN noise model, ECAC Doc 29, observed departures; aggregates only, local use). Fuel and capacity models are not connected.
